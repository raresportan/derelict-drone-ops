# Derelict Drone Ops feature map

Everything the game does, how a player reaches it, and what verifies it.

- **Title menu.** The app opens on the LACUNA title screen, offering terminal orientation, a new contract, the archive and the mission data, and the option to resume a contract once there's a save.
  - ✅ boots › "The title menu offers terminal orientation and a new contract"
  - ✅ campaign › "After a restart the menu offers to continue from site 1"
- **A screen that fits.** The schematic fills its window once the tube has warmed up, and every control can be clicked where it's drawn.
  - ✅ boots › "The map fills its window, measured after the warm-up"
  - ✅ boots › "Every control can be clicked where it's drawn"
- **Terminal orientation.** Type 1; the SCT-7 operator manual on a simulated hulk, where a highlighted line gives one order at a time, and each step moves on once it's done. Undocking ends it and marks it done on the menu.
  - ✅ tutorial › "The tutorial starts at its first step"
  - ✅ tutorial › "Doing step ${step} moves on to step ${step + 1}"
  - ✅ tutorial › "All three drones come home"
  - ✅ tutorial › "Undocking ends orientation and returns to the menu, marked done"
- **The intro.** A first campaign opens with Cyberdime's transmission, the crew's SOS on a scope, a hard cut to SIGNAL LOST, an orbital pass over Lacuna and the hopper's landing: title cards on the screen, the log typed slowly, and sound cues when sound is on. Enter skips it, and 5 on the menu replays it.
  - ❌ the cards, scenes and sound are checked by a unit test of the timeline (game.test.ts) and by eye on journey screenshots; no journey reads the canvas or hears audio.
  - ✅ campaign › "A first campaign opens with Cyberdime's transmission"
  - ✅ campaign › "The intro moves through the SOS to the moon"
  - ✅ campaign › "Enter skips the rest into site 1, on the Surface"
- **Boarding a site.** Type 2; the hopper lands at a generated site on Lacuna. Drones move with `nav` through open doors, survey rooms, gather salvage and fuel, and bank it in the dock.
  - ✅ campaign › "The Surface's opening beat sets the scene"
  - ❌ moving, gathering and banking are covered by unit tests (game.test.ts) and the tutorial journey, not by a campaign journey on a generated ship.
- **Drone cams.** Each drone's card shows a live feed of what it's doing: a corridor rushing past while it moves, a slow pan when idle, the bay when docked, a claw on a crate while gathering and a scrolling terminal while reading (both with a progress bar), a striped door when blocked, WREN's scan sweep, BUOY's decoy rings, a lifeform's silhouette, and static when the drone is lost. Every working cam breaks up now and then: a few seconds of heavy static and torn lines, at random and on its own schedule.
  - ✅ tutorial › "A gathering drone's cam shows the job's progress bar filling"
  - ❌ the other scenes are checked by eye on screenshots; no journey reads their pixels. The interference's timing is unit tested (cam.test.ts), its look by eye.
- **Background sound.** With sound on, a boarding has a low hull hum and the odd creak, servos while drones move, a grinding clank while one gathers, data chirps while one reads, crackle in time with the cams' interference, and a motion-tracker beep that quickens and rises as a drone nears a seen or suspected lifeform.
  - ❌ the mix is unit tested (game.test.ts) and the app was played once with sound on and no errors; no journey hears audio.
- **Lifeforms and hazards.** Lifeforms hunt drones through open doors, closed doors stop them, purge valves kill them, radiation wears hulls down. On the map a room with a lifeform in sight is red, and a room where one was sensed (a motion contact, or a lifeform that slipped out of sight) is reddish, fading over 25 seconds unless a drone looks in first. While BUOY's decoy sounds, the rooms it can be heard in, through open doors and within its reach, pulse orange outward from BUOY; a closed door stops it there.
  - ✅ fight › "The lair is shut off: no open route to it"
  - ✅ fight › "A lifeform is detected once MOTH shares its room"
  - ✅ fight › "The lair glows red on the map while MOTH sees the lifeform"
  - ✅ fight › "MOTH's feed and the status line raise the alarm"
  - ✅ fight › "In the lair the lifeform wears MOTH's hull down"
  - ✅ fight › "Purging the lair kills the lifeform, and MOTH with it"
  - ✅ fight › "With MOTH gone the alarm clears"
  - ✅ fight › "With the lair seen empty, no room stays red"
  - ❌ the decoy's reach is unit tested (game.test.ts) and checked by eye on screenshots; no journey reads it off the map.
  - ✅ fight › "WREN, sent in after the purge, finds the lair empty"
  - ❌ radiation, the decoy and a lifeform stopped at a closed door are covered by unit tests only (game.test.ts covers doors; radiation and the decoy have none).
- **Drydock and hops.** Undocking reaches the drydock, where scrap buys repairs, rebuilds, upgrades and fuel; HOP flies to the next, harder site. RENAME gives a drone a new call sign of 3 to 8 characters there or during a boarding, and the name is saved with the contract (unit tested in game.test.ts and save.test.ts).
  - ✅ campaign › "Undocking reaches the drydock"
  - ✅ campaign › "Hopping flies to site 2"
  - ❌ prices and refusals are covered by unit tests (game.test.ts), not by a journey.
- **Saves.** The campaign saves at the drydock and survives a restart; a damaged save file is ignored rather than stopping the app.
  - ✅ campaign › "Continuing restores the drydock"
  - ✅ damaged-save › "A damaged save still opens on the title menu, printed in full"
  - ✅ damaged-save › "The damaged campaign isn't offered to continue"
  - ✅ damaged-save › "What wasn't damaged is kept: the best run and the finished tutorial"
  - ✅ damaged-save › "The next save replaces the damaged file with a whole one"
- **Function keys.** F1–F9 and the key row run the commands shown for the current screen.
  - ✅ keys › "The function keys follow the screen"
  - ✅ keys › "Each key's number is a narrow cell beside its label"
  - ✅ keys › "F3 runs the motion scanner"
  - ✅ keys › "The LABELS key answers a click"
- **Playable in a browser.** The same page runs on GitHub Pages, from a subpath and with no app bridge, keeping saves in local storage.
  - ✅ browser › "The build opens from a Pages-style subpath, its assets found"
  - ✅ browser › "A browser has no app bridge, so saves go to local storage"
  - ✅ browser › "Reaching the drydock saves the campaign in local storage"
  - ✅ browser › "After a reload the menu offers to continue"
  - ❌ runs in Electron's Chromium, not in Safari or Firefox; the live deploy itself is checked by hand.
- **The icon.** An amber CRT tile with a drone on a schematic (resources/icon.svg) is the app's dock and window icon and the web page's favicon.
  - ✅ browser › "The page shows the terminal icon as its favicon"
  - ❌ the dock and window icon are checked by eye; no journey reads them.
- **The story.** Powered terminals hold the crew's logs: 15 story logs in order by depth across the Surface, the Underworks and the Bore, and 30 crew logs. `read` puts a log in the archive, which outlasts every run; 4 on the menu lists and replays it.
  - ✅ tutorial › "Reading the training terminal prints Cyberdime's briefing"
  - ✅ tutorial › "The archive keeps the briefing the tutorial's terminal gave up"
  - ❌ story order, depth gating and never-twice are covered by unit tests (game.test.ts); no journey reads a story terminal on a campaign site.
- **The ending.** Lifting off the Bore floor, site 21, asks whether to DELIVER the canisters or BROADCAST the logs; the run then goes on into the deep sectors.
  - ❌ unit tests only (game.test.ts: the ending, the retry after a loss on site 21, the deep sectors); no journey plays 21 sites.
- **Story text loaded only when needed.** The intro, its scene, each region's logs and the ending are separate files, fetched when first needed; only the current and next region stay in memory.
  - ✅ browser › "At the menu no story text is loaded yet, nor bundled into the main script"
  - ✅ browser › "A campaign loads the intro and the Surface, and nothing deeper"
  - ❌ letting go of a region's text after leaving it is covered by a unit test only (game.test.ts).
